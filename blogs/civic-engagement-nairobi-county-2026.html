You Know How We're Always Saying the County Doesn't Listen to Us?

By Bonga Na Gava Community Update 2026

Hey Bonga Na Gava community,

You know how we're always saying the county doesn't listen to us? Well, that narrative might be changing. The Nairobi City County Government is actually writing a new rulebook on how they should engage with residents — officially called the Civic Education, Citizen Engagement and Public Participation Policy 2026. And the best part? They are actively asking for our input to build it.

This isn't just another boring government document to ignore. For us as young people, this is a rare chance to literally write the rules on how we want to be heard, how public forums should run, and how the county communicates with us moving forward.

Honestly, if you've ever found yourself saying:

  • "They never consult the youth."
  • "Public participation is just a PR stunt."
  • "The government really needs to engage us better."

This is our chance to define exactly what "better" looks like.

The Receipts

We didn't just tweet about it. We submitted formal public participation on the draft policy — and the county responded. Here's the proof, straight from the movement:

What Happens Next

The county is now building a policy that will decide how residents are consulted — on budgets, on bills, on the issues that shape our daily lives. The window to shape it is open, and it closes fast. What we say now becomes the baseline for every public forum, every town hall, every consultation from here on.

So breathe. Read it. Add your voice. This is our chance to define exactly what "better" looks like — and make sure City Hall actually delivers on it.
