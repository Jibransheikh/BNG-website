+254 75* *** 617		 Nairobi, KE FCMA As a young Kenyan, I feel that this Bill talks a lot about management, regulation, and infrastructure, but very little about the people who will live with the long term effects of these decisions. Forests are becoming smaller every year, climate change is affecting rainfall and food security, and many communities already struggle with water shortages. Because of this, forest laws should focus first on protection and restoration before commercial use. I am particularly worried about opening public forests to roads, pipelines, and other developments through Section 56. Even when projects begin as "public interest" developments, they can slowly lead to destruction of forests, human encroachment, and loss of biodiversity. Once forests are damaged, it takes decades to recover them. 8 May 2026

+254 11* *** 229		 Nairobi, KE Energy Policy This policy represents a major effort to reform how the country produces and uses power. And it is essentially built on principles of sustainability, equity, and economic development and seeks to aim for a just energy transition. However, a significant gap exists between these ambitions and the current reality, where over 60% of households still rely on biomass for cooking. While there is strong support for renewable sources like geothermal and wind, the system currently lacks the grid readiness and storage needed to fully integrate them. A major point of contention is the inclusion of fossil fuels, specifically coal for industrial use and natural gas as a transition fuel, which critics see as a contradiction to clean energy goals. 1 May 2026
