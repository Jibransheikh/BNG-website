About Us

Securing a Nature Positive 2030.

Bonga Na Gava is designed to bridge the gap between young Kenyans and the legislative tables where decisions are made. We facilitate the youth to formally present their views to the government on proposed laws, policies and regulations.

"Our mission is to get the voice of the youth and communities to the tables where the decisions are being made, to ensure inclusion and diversity of opinions brings out the best outcomes for our country."
Peter Moll, Founder

How it Works

  1. 01.

    A public participation opportunity opens

    A law, policy, bill, regulation, or other legal instrument is identified as open for public participation and the opportunity is uploaded onto the BNG system.

  2. 02.

    You receive an alert

    Registered BNG warriors and citizens receive an SMS alert with information about the issue under review, the opportunity to participate, and how to engage.

  3. 03.

    You choose topline comments

    Through a simple SMS flow, participants receive clear topline comments or engagement options that help break down the issue into understandable and actionable points.

  4. 04.

    You submit your views

    Using the SMS menu, participants can select and submit comments, concerns, recommendations, or suggestions on the issue under review.

  5. 05.

    Your views are captured

    Responses are collected and organised by BNG, creating a structured record of citizen contributions.

  6. 06.

    Stay in the loop

    Participants may receive reminders, deadline alerts, confirmations, and other relevant updates throughout the engagement period.

  7. 07.

    Voices become insights

    BNG reviews and analyses the contributions to identify key concerns, suggestions, recurring issues, and emerging trends. These insights help inform reports, dashboards, advocacy efforts, and memoranda submitted to relevant government authorities.

  8. 08.

    The loop is closed

    Where information is available, participants are updated on outcomes, key next steps, and links to final memoranda or other outputs.
