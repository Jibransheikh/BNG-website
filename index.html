About Us

Securing a Nature-Positive 2030.

Bonga Na Gava is designed to bridge the gap between young Kenyans and the legislative tables where decisions are made. We facilitate the youth to formally present their views to the government on proposed policies.

"Our mission is to get the voice of the youth and communities to the tables where the decisions are being made, to ensure inclusion and diversity of opinions brings out the best outcomes for our country."
— Peter Moll, Founder

Did You Know?

Africa has the youngest population in the world, yet is the most vulnerable to climate change. By reviewing documents like the Draft Forest Conservation Management Act, you directly influence the laws dictating our environmental survival.
